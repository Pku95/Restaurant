Our story

A grill room built around one fire

We opened in 2018 with a small menu, a charcoal sigri and a dining room we wanted to sit in ourselves. Not much has changed.

How we cook

Four steps, none of them fast

Mughlai cooking rewards patience. These are the four things our kitchen will not compromise on.

  1. 01

    Charcoal first

    We build the fire before service and cook kebabs over live charcoal.

  2. 02

    Marinate overnight

    Meat, yoghurt, spice and aromatics get time to develop their full flavour.

  3. 03

    Seal the kacchi

    Aged basmati, mutton, saffron and kewra are layered and slow-cooked in sealed handis.

  4. 04

    Finish by hand

    Every plate is checked, garnished and served while it is still at its best.
